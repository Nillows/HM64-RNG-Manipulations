Before you start

  • Match your file. Check Your game file needs above. A file that differs (another weather, a horse, the dog somewhere else) puts the dog on paths the tables do not have.
  • The route. Everything from power-on to the end of the pier decides which dog path you get: play it the same, as fast as possible, every time. The setup and the route are in the guide; your keys, your timing and practice are on Settings; every setting is explained in the README.
  • New to it? Start with Dog practice: no console needed. Where do the numbers come from? How the odds are worked out · How it works, in pictures.
  • Is my file right? After a few Lucky Timer attempts with a Space tap at a bite, open Setup & review → L5 → Is the app working?: bite taps that keep fitting no dog path (off the tables) mean the file, the menus or the route do not match the lab's.
  • A desktop browser with a keyboard. Cast and reel on the controller; the keyboard is only for the tool.
