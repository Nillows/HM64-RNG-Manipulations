HM64 Power Nut Tool v32.2

Catch the Power Nut from the ocean pier in Harvest Moon 64 (USA, NTSC) on a real N64.

The dog on the beach shows where the game's random numbers are. The tables behind every page come from an emulator lab that played every dog path and every cast moment, and they hold for one kind of game file: pick the morning and the file you have. Each page is a single file with only that file's tables: save it (right-click the link → Save link as…) and it works offline, with nothing sent anywhere.

☀ Sunny morning

The morning you play is sunny and starts with no cutscene when you wake up. Any such day works.

Your game file needs
  • No horse yet. Anything that moves on the route and is not in the lab's file (a horse, farm animals) can draw random numbers before the beach and send you down another path.
  • The fishing rod in Jack's hand (equipped), not in the rucksack.
  • The dog left on the beach the day before, in the far south-west corner (bottom-left on screen), and not carried anywhere on the day you play. From the end of the pier it sits at the lower-left edge of the screen.
  • Saved in bed in the first diary slot (another slot means extra menu presses: later than the tables).
How to make it
  1. The day before: carry the dog to the beach, put it down in the farthest south-west (bottom-left) corner and leave it there.
  2. Check the rod is in Jack's hand and you have no horse.
  3. Go home, go to bed and save in the first diary slot.
  4. The next morning must be sunny with no wake-up cutscene. If it rains, reload the day before and sleep again.

Your game file needs
  • You own the horse. Owning it changes the random numbers at the ranch on the way, whether or not you see it, so a file with a horse needs these tables and not the no-horse ones.
  • The horse left on the farm far from the house door, where it never comes on screen while Jack walks from the door to the farm exit. A horse that comes on screen draws more random numbers (and can get in the way) and sends you down another path.
  • The lab's file has the pony (not yet grown). A grown horse has not been tested.
  • The fishing rod in Jack's hand (equipped), not in the rucksack.
  • The dog left on the beach the day before, in the far south-west corner (bottom-left on screen), and not carried anywhere on the day you play. From the end of the pier it sits at the lower-left edge of the screen.
  • Saved in bed in the first diary slot (another slot means extra menu presses: later than the tables).
How to make it
  1. The day before: carry the dog to the beach, put it down in the farthest south-west (bottom-left) corner and leave it there.
  2. Make sure the horse is on the farm far from the house door, out of sight of the walk from the door to the farm exit (in the lab's file it is at x 449, z −339 in game units).
  3. Check the rod is in Jack's hand.
  4. Go home, go to bed and save in the first diary slot.
  5. The next morning must be sunny with no wake-up cutscene. If it rains, reload the day before and sleep again.

Before you start

  • Match your file. Open your circumstance above and check Your game file needs. A file that differs (another weather, a horse, the dog somewhere else) puts the dog on paths the tables do not have.
  • The route. Everything from power-on to the end of the pier decides which dog path you get: play it the same, as fast as possible, every time. The full route, every setting and the keys are in the guide.
  • Is my file right? After a few Lucky Timer attempts with a Space tap at a bite, open Setup & review → L5 → Is the app working?. Bite taps that keep fitting no dog path (off the tables) mean the file, the menus or the route do not match the lab's.
  • A desktop browser with a keyboard. Cast and reel on the controller; the keyboard is only for the tool.
