Your timing

Each dot is one thing measured about you: left = early, right = late, top = steady, bottom = loose. A filled dot has enough data. Reacting to a cue you can't time (Jack stopping, a bite) and pressing on a countdown you see coming (CAST) are different timings, so they stay separate.

Set by hand

Moves only the CAST cue, for your B-press habit. Auto = minus the median of your B presses (cast practice + real casts with the cast tap).
Auto = your B presses' spread + ±60 ms for the start tap + how well the console offset is known (±60 ms until the Lucky Timer has learned it). The Lucky Timer plans every cast for it and the Casting tables work their odds out with it.

Practice

The Lucky Timer's two drills, with the same timings and the same records. Press and hold your start key ( — or a controller button, or the mouse on the picture); Esc stops.
