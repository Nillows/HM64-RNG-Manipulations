Top-decking the Power Nut

Catching the Power Nut is like drawing one chosen card off the top of a shuffled deck — a deck that only holds still for a moment, because it reshuffles every time the game's random number changes. Try it: pick your card, then draw blind.
The rules of this deck (and the game they stand for)
  • One card out, 51 left. 1 in 51 is the game's base chance of the Power Nut per catch.
  • The deck flickers. Each moment a new card is on top, in lockstep with the unseen random number. In the game a moment is one frame, 1/30 of a second; here it lasts 0.33 s (10× slower) unless you switch to the game's own speed.
  • It listens to a real dog line. Each deck is one arrival at the beach: a dog line of your game file, drawn by how often the route reaches it. Your goal card is on top exactly on the frames where a cast on that line lands the Power Nut on the first bite.
  • Drawing reshuffles. A cast changes the random numbers, so every draw shuffles the drawn card back in: a new deck. Leaving the beach gives a new deck too.
  • A reaching hand speeds it up. While you hold the button down the deck shuffles twice as fast (fishing draws more random numbers than standing still); with your arm at your side it goes at its usual pace. You draw when you let go.
