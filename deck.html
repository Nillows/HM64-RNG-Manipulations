Top-decking the Power Nut

Catching the Power Nut is like drawing one chosen card from a deck of 51 — except that you cannot draw whenever you like. You cast, you wait, and each bite lights up one face-down card for a third of a second: let go while your card is lit.
The rules of this deck (and the fishing they stand for)
  • One card out, 51 left. 1 in 51 is the game's base chance of the Power Nut per catch.
  • Start = Jack has stopped at the end of the pier. The clock runs; Jack lies down after 40 s if you have not cast.
  • One cast per attempt. Whatever comes up (a fish, an empty line, Jack lying down), the attempt is over. The next attempt is a new arrival at the pier with a different dog — a second cast on the same visit would depend on what the first one did, and the tables do not guess at that.
  • Press and hold the deck = cast (B on the controller). The game frame you press on (1/30 s) decides everything after it: the deck reshuffles every frame, so a frame earlier or later is another cast with other bites.
  • Watch the big card: it is your bobber. At each bite it glows (and Jack's rod dips on it) for 0.33 s, and one face-down card lights up with it: the card that bite brings.
  • Each bite lights one face-down card for 0.33 s — the time the game gives you to react to a bite. Let go while it is lit and you catch that card. Hold on through a bite and the next one comes later; let go when nothing is lit and the line comes back empty. Which bite is your card, you cannot see.
  • Real casts. Every cast here is a real cast from the lab's tables (Sunny · No horse, casts up to 40 s after Jack stops, every bite to the end of the data), at your exact frame. Real speed: nothing is slowed down.
